Account Deletion

Delete Your DOT Account

DOT gives you control over your personal information and account.

How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account directly inside the DOT app by navigating to:

Profile → Settings → Delete Account

Follow the prompts in the app to permanently delete your account.

What Happens When Your Account Is Deleted

When your account is deleted, DOT removes personal information associated with your account, including:

  • Your profile information
  • Your profile photo
  • Your trips
  • Your requests
  • Your train rooms
  • Your notes
  • Your notifications
  • Your shared trip experiences, where applicable

Data Retention

Some information may be retained temporarily where necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or operational purposes. Any retained data will be handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy and applicable laws.

Need Help?

If you cannot access your DOT account, you may request account deletion by contacting:

support@dotapp.ng

Please include the email address associated with your DOT account so we can identify the account to be deleted.
