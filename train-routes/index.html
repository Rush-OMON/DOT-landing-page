Train Routes

Traveling Lagos, Ibadan, or Abeokuta by train?

DOT helps passengers on Nigeria's busiest rail corridor find and match with trusted travel companions before their trip, so the journey feels less random and a lot more comfortable.

Routes DOT covers

Built for the Lagos–Ibadan–Abeokuta corridor.

01

Lagos ↔ Ibadan

One of Nigeria's most traveled train routes. Whether you're commuting for work or heading home, DOT helps you know who might be sitting near you before you board.

02

Lagos ↔ Abeokuta

Abeokuta sits along the same standard gauge line connecting to Lagos. DOT lets you match with fellow passengers making the same stop.

03

Ibadan ↔ Abeokuta

Traveling between these two cities on the rail corridor is easier when you already know who's on the trip with you.

Busy routes mean crowded trains and total strangers.

Lagos, Ibadan, and Abeokuta trains carry some of the highest passenger volumes in the country. DOT gives you a way to travel with more intention, not less randomness, on a route you already take.

FAQ

Questions about these routes.

Does DOT cover the Lagos to Ibadan train route?

Yes. Lagos–Ibadan is one of the most active routes on DOT, since it runs along Nigeria's standard gauge rail line and is one of the most traveled train corridors in the country.

Can I use DOT for the Abeokuta train route?

Yes. Abeokuta sits on the same Lagos–Ibadan rail corridor, and DOT supports matching for passengers travelling to, from, or through Abeokuta.

Does DOT sell train tickets?

No. DOT is not a ticketing platform. You book your train ticket as usual through official Nigerian Railway Corporation (NRC) channels, and use DOT to find compatible passengers for that same trip.

How do I find a travel companion for my Lagos, Ibadan, or Abeokuta trip?

Download DOT, create your trip with your route and travel date, and DOT will help you find and match with other passengers taking the same journey.
